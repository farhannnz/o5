About the class

What is Pilates?

Pilates at O5 is taught on reformers and chairs in a light-filled room with arched mirrors. Every movement is slow, deliberate and guided, so you build strength from the inside out.

  • Best forCore strength, posture, toning
  • LevelBeginner to advanced
  • FormatSmall group sessions
Why it works

What your body gains

Deep core strength

Train the stabilising muscles around your spine and hips.

Better posture

Alignment work that helps you stand taller without effort.

Strength with grace

Low-impact, joint-friendly work that tones the whole body.

Your first class

What to expect

  • A short breath and alignment warm-up
  • Guided reformer or chair sequences with spring resistance
  • Options to make each movement easier or harder
  • A calm cool-down and stretch

If you have an injury or a medical condition, please tell us when you join the list.

Good to know

Common questions

Do I need to be strong or flexible?

No. Pilates builds both. Your instructor will adjust springs and positions to suit your level.

What should I wear?

Fitted, stretchy clothing and grip socks. Avoid zips and loose jewellery that can catch on the equipment.

Is it safe with a back problem?

Pilates is often recommended for back care, but please tell us about any injury or condition when you join the list so we can guide you well.
