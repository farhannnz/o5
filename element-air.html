The element

Air in practice

Air is the element of breath and lift. It is the feeling of the ribs widening on an inhale and the spine lengthening as you let go on an exhale.

In aerial yoga the silk hammock holds your weight so the body can open without effort. You hang, fold and float, and every breath creates a little more space between the vertebrae.

  • Decompresses the spine and releases the lower back
  • Deepens breathing and calms the nervous system
  • Builds a playful sense of lightness in the body
