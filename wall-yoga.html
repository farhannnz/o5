About the class

What is Wall Yoga?

Wall yoga uses ropes anchored to the wall to support classic poses. The ropes take the strain, so you can hold longer, find real alignment and stretch safely.

  • Best forAlignment, balance, back and hip mobility
  • LevelIdeal for beginners and recovery
  • FormatSmall group sessions
Why it works

What your body gains

Alignment

Learn what a well-stacked spine and pelvis feel like.

Supported back release

Open the back and hips with the ropes holding you steady.

Grounded calm

Slow holds and steady breath settle the mind.

Your first class

What to expect

  • Gentle mobilising on the mat
  • Learning to set up and use the wall ropes
  • Supported standing, folding and hanging poses
  • Slow stretches and a final rest

If you have an injury or a medical condition, please tell us when you join the list.

Good to know

Common questions

Is wall yoga good for beginners?

It is one of the friendliest ways to start. The ropes give you support and confidence in every pose.

Can it help with stiffness?

Many people use it for tight backs, hips and hamstrings. Tell us about any injury so we can guide you well.
