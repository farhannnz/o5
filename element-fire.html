The element

Fire in practice

Fire is transformation. It is the slow burn you feel in the centre of the body as you hold a position, the discipline of showing up and the energy that builds rep by rep.

On the reformer, springs and controlled resistance light the deep core muscles. The work is never rushed. It is steady, deliberate heat that leaves you strong rather than drained.

  • Builds deep core strength
  • Tones the whole body with low-impact resistance
  • Grows stamina, discipline and drive
