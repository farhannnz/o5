Earth in practice

Earth is steadiness. It is the floor beneath your feet and the wall at your back, the part of you that stays calm while everything else moves.

Wall yoga uses ropes anchored to the wall to hold the body in classic poses. With that support you can stay longer, find true alignment and stretch with confidence.

  • Improves balance and alignment
  • Safe, supported stretching for back and hips
  • Ideal for beginners and recovery
