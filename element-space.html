The element

Space in practice

Space is the quiet that holds everything else. It is the pause between two repetitions, the openness in the chest, the moment the mind finally stops chattering.

In Pilates we work with space through precision and posture: lengthening, aligning and leaving room around each joint so that movement feels clean and unforced.

  • Refines posture and alignment
  • Sharpens focus and body awareness
  • Creates room for mental stillness
